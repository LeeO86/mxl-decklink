# Changelog

## 1.0.5

Interlaced video (`PAL`, `NTSC`, `HD1080i50`, `HD1080i5994`) now follows the shared convention of the MXL media functions (SPECIFICATION §3.2.2): one field per grain at twice the frame rate, grain 2k = the first field in time of TAI frame k, grain 2k + 1 = the second; the flow definition keeps `grain_rate` = the frame rate and the real `interlace_mode`. Progressive modes are unchanged.

- **Input (SDI → MXL):** an interlaced capture wrote one grain per frame at the field-rate index, holding frame lines 0–539 of the woven frame (the top half with both fields, cut to the grain size); every odd index stayed invalid. Every reader that follows the convention (multiviewer, webrtc-monitor, srt and 2110 egress) showed the top half stretched, with combing. Now the frame index k comes from the frame's start (one frame period before its timestamp) at the frame rate, and the bridge writes grain 2k from the first field's lines and 2k + 1 from the other lines, by row (v210, and 8-bit YUV after the expansion; for `video/v210a` the fill lines). `mxl_decklink_grains_committed_total` and `grains_committed` count grains: two per interlaced frame, one per progressive frame; `frames_total` counts frames.
- **Field order:** `NTSC` is lower field first (`bmdLowerFieldFirst`), so its flow definition and NMOS flow and receiver caps say `interlaced_bff`; they said `interlaced_tff` for every interlaced mode. `PAL` and 1080i stay `interlaced_tff`.
- **ANC of an interlaced input** runs at the field rate: the `video/smpte291` flow is declared at twice the frame rate (50/1 for 1080i50, 60000/1001 for 1080i59.94), field 1's packets go to grain 2k and field 2's to grain 2k + 1, the index of the video field, with the RFC 8331 `F` bits 0b10 (field 1) and 0b11 (field 2), as mxl-test-player 1.2.3 does; progressive grains keep 0b00. When only one field of a frame has packets, the other gets an empty grain; a frame without ANC packets writes no ANC grain. The field follows from the packet's SMPTE line number (line 0, unknown, counts as field 1). The NMOS ANC flow (`grain_rate`) and the ANC receiver caps follow the MXL flow definition: the field rate for interlaced modes (video keeps the frame rate). Before, the packets were written at the field-rate index into a data flow declared at the frame rate, about twice the TAI index, so readers asking for the current index found nothing. The output does not read ANC, so there is no reader to change.
- **Output (MXL → SDI):** an interlaced flow was read one field per output frame into the top half of the DeckLink frame (the rest stale), the read position advanced at the field rate (half of what the source writes, so it fell behind and skipped ahead), and the audio per frame was half a frame (960 samples at 1080i50). Now the reader reads frame k as grains 2k and 2k + 1 and weaves them with line parity `(index & 1) ^ bff`; read position, preroll, read offset, catch-up and slips count frames, the sync-group wait waits for the second field's time, and the audio per frame is 48000 / frame rate (1920 at 25 frames/s). A frame with an invalid field repeats the last frame (`source_invalid`), without waiting for the second field when the first is invalid. A flow definition that cannot be read is read as progressive and logged (`mxl_flow_def_unreadable`, warning). A re-created flow that changes between progressive and interlaced restarts the channel. `output_channel_started` logs `interlaced`.
- **Web UI:** the MXL tab now maps an interlaced flow to its mode: 1080 lines at 25/1 to `HD1080i50`, at 30000/1001 to `HD1080i5994`, 576 lines at 25/1 to `PAL`, 486 lines at 30000/1001 to `NTSC`. It looked for `HD1080i25` and `HD1080i2997`, which do not exist, and offered no mode.
- **Mock card:** in interlaced modes the counter band of the odd lines is mid grey, so each field can be told apart; new `MOCK_PLAYBACK_FRAME_FILE` writes every 25th frame an output is given to a file.
- New unit tests: field split and weave for tff and bff (every line back in place), the frame index from the frame's start and the field timestamps at 1080i50 and 1080i59.94, the newest complete frame of a field head, ANC line → field, an interlaced flow written field by field and read back as woven frames (PAL tff, NTSC bff), an output playing an interlaced flow at the frame rate with woven frames and a frame's worth of audio, an input channel capturing PAL from a fake card (field grains at 2k and 2k + 1, two grains per frame, ANC per field at 50/1 with F bits), the RFC 8331 F bits, and a frame with an invalid first field returned at once.

## 1.0.4

- An output channel re-opens its MXL readers when the writer re-creates a flow under the same id. MXL then gives the flow a new data file, and a read past the old head returns `MXL_ERR_FLOW_INVALID` (dmf-mxl/mxl@218ddaa `lib/internal/src/PosixDiscreteFlowReader.cpp:96-104`, `244-254`). Before, the channel treated that like any failed read: it repeated the last frame and counted a drop, but every read of the stale reader first waited `CHx_READER_TIMEOUT_MS` twice (sync group, then grain). Only a preroll reset (25 late or dropped DeckLink frames in 10 s) re-opened the reader; when the card did not report late frames, the SDI output stayed frozen until the channel was restarted. Now the callback takes the reader out of the sync group and stops reading it; the supervisor releases it and opens it again for the same flow id at once, then after 100 ms, 200 ms, 500 ms and every 1 s while the flow is missing. Video repeats the last frame and the re-created audio flow plays silence until the new reader is open; these repeats count in `mxl_decklink_frames_repeated_total{reason="flow_reopen"}` and never trigger a preroll reset. A flow missing for more than 1 s marks the channel `degraded` until it is back. A re-created flow with another format, rate, line size or channel count restarts the channel instead. New counter `mxl_decklink_flow_reopens_total` (one per re-created flow) and log events `mxl_flow_recreated` (once per event), `mxl_flow_reopen_failed` (first failed open of an event), `mxl_flow_reopened` (with `attempts` and `after_ms`) and `mxl_flow_recreated_format_changed`. mxl-st2110-gateway, FlowXer, the multiviewer and replay already re-open on this error.
- Output channels open their MXL readers on MXL instances of their own, one per domain the output reads, also for this function's own domain. MXL keeps one reader per flow id and instance and hands it out again while any holder keeps it, so two outputs reading the same input flow on the shared instance could not re-open it. This costs one MXL instance per output channel and domain it reads (an idle inotify watcher thread and a few file descriptors). Input channels write and do not read; they are unchanged.
- An output's video and audio can come from different MXL domains (different functions). Each IS-05 receiver now keeps its own domain: the video receiver sets the output's video domain and each audio receiver its audio flow's domain (`ChannelConfig::readerDomainPath`, new `AudioFlowConfig::readerDomainPath`), and the output opens one MXL instance per domain it reads. Before, every activation of any of the output's receivers set the one domain of the whole output (also in 1.0.3, `src/nmos/routing.cpp`), so with the video from one function and the audio from another every bring-up failed (`mxlCreateFlowReader (audio …) failed with status 2`, `MXL_ERR_FLOW_NOT_FOUND`, hardware trial on small, platform record D-225). The sync group takes readers of any instance, so the audio from the other domain still waits with the video. A reader never creates another function's domain directory: a route to a domain that does not exist yet waits until it does (bring-up retries). New unit test: video from domain A and audio from domain B, both flows re-created.
- A source stall no longer resets the SDI output. When an output's source stops writing but its flow stays (for example a fabrics mirror whose remote origin restarts for a few seconds), the channel repeats the last frame and plays silence on the stalled audio flow's channels, for as long as the stall lasts, without a preroll reset. Before, every DeckLink completion callback waited `CHx_READER_TIMEOUT_MS` for the sync group, again for the grain, and the audio callback a third time; the card ran out of frames and reported them late or dropped, and 25 of those in 10 s reset the channel (healthy → init → healthy). On small a 5 s stall gave 2–3 resets, 67–70 dropped and 37–44 late frames. Now a read that finds nothing from a source whose head has not moved for two frame periods (timed across calls, so a live writer of 20 ms blocks is not taken for stalled) is stalled: it leaves the sync group and is read without waiting until its head moves again; an audio block the resumed source has not written yet is read again, not played as silence. A read in a completion callback waits at most `CHx_READER_TIMEOUT_MS` and never past the time the card still holds frames for ((`CHx_OUTPUT_PREROLL_GRAINS` − 1) frames less a quarter frame, at least 4 ms; consecutive waits share that deadline); the audio callback uses the same deadline. A source that writes but later than that repeats the frame instead of making the card late, and the read position slips one frame so the next read gets that grain: a source whose latency grows (a mirror back with more buffering, a card a few ppm fast) keeps playing instead of repeating one frame for good while the channel shows healthy. When the source's newest grain stays more than the read offset + 2 grains ahead for 1 s (latency shrank, or the card runs slow), the read position skips ahead to it; a read out of the history skips at once; a burst of completions after an output-side delay skips nothing. The audio read position moves with every slip and skip (one block per grain), so lip sync holds; an audio read out of the history re-aligns to the video. A source late for more than 1 s marks the channel `degraded` (log `source_late`, `source_late_ended`). New per-output setting `CHx_READ_OFFSET_GRAINS` (default 0): read that many grains behind the newest grain, 1–2 for mirrored sources with fabrics lag. It is capped at bring-up so that preroll + offset stays inside the video ring (grain count − 3) and each audio ring (buffer length / 2), log `read_offset_capped`. Audio stays on the video's TAI grid through a stall, so it is in sync when the source resumes (before, a stall longer than the audio history left the audio silent until a restart). The preroll reset still counts only DeckLink frames displayed late or dropped. A stall longer than 1 s marks the channel `degraded` until it ends.
- **Meaning change of `mxl_decklink_frames_dropped_total` for outputs:** from 1.0.4 it counts only output-side losses, frames the card dropped and frames that could not be scheduled. Before, it also counted every frame the output repeated because no new grain could be read; those are now in `mxl_decklink_frames_repeated_total`. Dashboards or alerts on output drops see far lower numbers for the same source trouble. Inputs are unchanged.
- Repeated frames are counted apart from frames the output lost: new counter `mxl_decklink_frames_repeated_total{reason}` with `source_stall`, `source_late`, `flow_reopen` and `read_error`, and `frames_repeated` in `/statusz`, `/api/status` and the web dashboard. For outputs, `mxl_decklink_frames_dropped_total` and `frames_dropped` now count only output-side losses: frames the card dropped and frames that could not be scheduled (before, they also counted every repeated frame). New counter `mxl_decklink_source_stalls_total{essence="video"|"audio"}`, log events `source_stall` (warning, once a stall lasted 200 ms) and `source_stall_ended` (`duration_ms`, `frames_repeated` of the whole stall). New unit tests with the fake output (which reports a frame scheduled after its display time as late): video and audio stall for 2.5 s, the output keeps playing 50 frames/s with the last frame and silence, is `degraded` after 1 s and `healthy` after the source resumes, and nothing is late, dropped or reset; a source whose latency grows by one grain every 300 ms to 4 grains keeps playing live grains and the read position catches up when it shrinks to 0, with audio block − video grain unchanged after the slips and after the catch-up (stamped grains and samples); three 60 ms output-side delays skip no grain; `CHx_READ_OFFSET_GRAINS=64` is capped and plays; a source 2 grains late plays without repeats with `CHx_READ_OFFSET_GRAINS=2`; an audio writer of whole 20 ms blocks at the read position is never taken for stalled and no block goes silent.
- IS-05 routes survive a restart. Every accepted activation of an MXL receiver or sender is saved to `state/connections.json` next to the configuration file (`$CONFIG_DIR/state/connections.json` by default; written atomically, not part of config export/import): `master_enable`, `mxl_domain_id`, the domain's directory, `mxl_flow_id`, and `sender_id` / `receiver_id`. At the next start the node applies them before the channels start and shows them in IS-05 `/active` and `/staged` and in the IS-04 `subscription` (`sender_id` / `receiver_id`, `active`), so the outputs come back on their routes without a controller. The file is written durably (fsync of the temp file and of the directory after the rename); an empty or broken file is renamed to `connections.json.bad` and logged instead of being overwritten with one route. Saved routes win over flow ids from the environment or the config file. Receivers wait for a domain or flow that is not there yet (the function that writes it may start later): a domain not found by id is read at its saved directory once that exists (log `nmos_connection_restored` with `waiting`). A saved route that the activation checks reject comes up with `master_enable` false and its flow id kept (log `restored_activation_disabled` with the reason), as mxl-st2110-gateway does (`restored_activation_disabled`); one for a sender or receiver that no longer exists is skipped (`restored_activation_skipped`). Senders restore `master_enable` only; their flow ids stay this function's own. New setting `NMOS_RESUME_CONNECTIONS` (default `true`); new log events `nmos_connection_saved`, `nmos_connection_restored`, `route_state_loaded`, `route_state_invalid`, `route_state_write_failed`. Before, the output came up without routes after a restart and SDI showed nothing until the platform activated them again (D-225), because the environment pins the flow ids and the domain was never saved. New unit tests (save and load, a restart that restores an output with video and audio from different domains, waiting and disabled restores) and an integration step in `tests/integration/nmos-smoke.sh` (route, restart, check `/active`).
- New unit tests with a fake DeckLink output and a real MXL domain: a video flow removed for 1.5 s and re-created (output never stops, channel `degraded` then `healthy`, one re-open, no restart; the 1.0.3 channel scheduled 16 frames in those 1.5 s and never showed the new flow), and an audio flow of the sync group re-created at once.
- An output comes up at once on a mirrored source. The preroll asked for grains that a mirror a grain or two behind (fabrics lag), or a mirror the route had just created, did not have, so every bring-up from such a source failed (`preroll_grain_unavailable`, `init` → `failed`, backoff) and the output started only after 1.6–1.7 s (hardware trial on small; `CHx_READ_OFFSET_GRAINS=2` did not help). Now the preroll starts no later than the source's newest grain allows, less the read offset; it passes over grains the source never wrote and waits for one it has not written yet, and fails only when nothing can be shown within `CHx_READER_TIMEOUT_MS` + (preroll + 3) frames. The audio starts with the grain of the first frame shown, also when the preroll jumped to a new mirror's first grain or passed over grains its writer marked invalid. A new session no longer repeats the previous session's last frame (it was kept across bring-ups, so a re-bring-up on a source without grains showed the old picture). `preroll_grain_unavailable` now carries `head_index` and `read_offset_grains`. New unit tests: a source 2 grains late, created as the output starts, with read offsets 0 and 2: healthy within 400 ms, no failed bring-up; lip sync after a preroll on a new mirror whose first grain lands during the preroll and on a source whose first grains are invalid; a re-bring-up on a source without grains shows nothing of the previous session; a slice-by-slice writer that jumps ahead.
- An output that resumes after a stall goes to the source's newest grain at once. A source that resumed at the current time left a gap of grains it never wrote; the first read after the stall read one of them (marked invalid), repeated the frame (`frames_repeated{reason="read_error"}` +1) and was not logged. The same for a read that fell out of the history: the newest grain is shown in that frame, and when that grain is not complete yet (a writer that commits slice by slice) the read position still moves on instead of falling out of the history at every frame. A grain that did not come within the wait but is older than the newest one is read once more before it counts as a gap.
- Read deadline: a completion call is taken as on time only when it came while no completion callback ran. Before, a short callback that followed long waits re-anchored the deadline on a call that had been queued, and a source slower than real time made the card show frames late (seen in the new log test).
- More log lines: every stall when it ends (`source_stall_ended` with `stalls`, `duration_ms` and `frames_repeated`, at most one line a second; stalls under 200 ms were only counted before); runs of late grains (`source_late` once a run has 3 late repeats less than 1 s apart, `source_late_ended` with `frames_repeated` and `read_position_slipped_grains`); catch-ups (`read_position_caught_up`); repeats for invalid grains or read errors (`grain_read_error`); frames the card showed late or dropped, or that could not be scheduled (`output_frames_lost`). The channel is `degraded` while no grain came in time for 1 s (late repeats only); a source late now and then stays `healthy`.
- The read offset is visible: log `read_offset_applied` (`requested`, `effective`) at every bring-up, `read_offset_grains` per channel in `/api/status` and `/statusz`, gauge `mxl_decklink_read_offset_grains`.
- Read-only report of how the card signals each SDI output: `smpte_level` (3G-SDI Level A or B, `bmdDeckLinkConfigSMPTELevelAOutput`), `supports_smpte_level_a`, `output_1080p_as_psf` and `link_configuration`, in `GET /api/card` (`status.sdi_output` per sub-device), in `/statusz` per output channel and in the log line `sdi_output_config` at every output bring-up. On small an output configured as HD1080p50 showed as 1080i50 on a receiver; this process sets none of these flags, so the driver default applies (likely Level B). No setting changes them.
- New setting `CHx_SDI_LEVEL` (`A`, `B` or `driver`; default `A`, owner decision). Before an output starts, `A` sets `bmdDeckLinkConfigSMPTELevelAOutput` and, for HD modes, the single link output (`bmdDeckLinkConfigSDIOutputLinkConfiguration`; the card on small reported Level B on a dual link, which a receiver showed as 1080i50); `B` sets Level B; `driver` changes nothing. A card without Level A support keeps the driver's setting (`sdi_level_not_set`, warning); Level A set but the single link refused logs `sdi_level_partial` (warning); otherwise `sdi_level_set`. The output holds the configuration interface for its playback session (the SDK may drop changes when it is released) and reads the values back after the output is enabled (`sdi_output_config`). `sdi_output` in `/api/card` and `/statusz` shows the values read back afterwards, plus `requested_level`. Not written to the Desktop Video preferences.
- An output comes up on an idle mirror (the flow exists, no grain yet: `head_index` 0). The preroll now waits up to 1 s for the first grain, then until the source has written that grain + read offset + preroll grains, and starts there with the audio aligned and the read offset in place (no late repeats to build it up); before, it guessed a position from head 0 and failed the bring-up (`preroll_grain_unavailable` twice, healthy after 0.87 s on small). - A source stall costs no output frames. The read deadline now comes from the card's playback clock (`GetScheduledStreamTime`) instead of the time of the completion call, and keeps a margin of a third of a frame (at least 8 ms, was a quarter frame, at least 4 ms). A card that calls back a few ms after a frame completed made the waits at a stall's start and end run past the frame's display time: on small one frame dropped at a stall's start and 2 late + 1 dropped at its end (`output_frames_lost`). New in the fake card of the unit tests: a 4 ms call latency and the playback clock; the stall test now requires 0 late frames (the round-3 deadline gave 2). Each bring-up logs how the clock steps (`stream_time_granularity`); a clock that steps in whole frames is combined with the estimate from the completion calls.
- A grain the source marked invalid (a player's gap grain after a freeze) repeats the frame with reason `source_invalid` and logs `source_invalid_grain` at info level; it was counted and logged as a read error (`grain_read_error`, warning).
- An output on an idle mirror waits for its first grain without failing. The fabrics replication of a mirror without demand can need more than 1 s for the first grain; the preroll gave up after 1 s (`preroll_no_grain_yet`, `channel_bringup_failed`, 500 ms retry; healthy 0.9 s later on small). Now it waits up to 10 s in state `init`, logs `preroll_no_grain_yet` (info) once after 1 s and `preroll_first_grain` with `waited_ms` when the grain comes, and comes up on that grain. Only with no grain after 10 s does the bring-up fail (`preroll_no_grain`, warning) and retry. New unit test: the first grain after 2.5 s, no failed bring-up, healthy within 0.5 s of the grain, audio in sync.

## 1.0.3

- No segmentation fault when one output channel releases its MXL readers while another output channel creates or releases its flow synchronization group. Output channels that read this function's own output domain (flow ids from the environment, or an IS-05 route to this function's own domain) share its MXL instance, and each kept its sync group on it. MXL removes a released reader from every sync group of the reader's instance (`Instance::releaseReader`, under the instance mutex), but it creates and releases sync groups without that mutex, and a group has no lock of its own (dmf-mxl/mxl@218ddaa `lib/internal/src/Instance.cpp:145-165`, `439-455`). With two or more such output channels, a channel restart (web UI, IS-05 activation, preroll reset, stop) next to another channel's bring-up or teardown could walk freed memory (SIGSEGV in `FlowSynchronizationGroup::removeReader`); the same race crashed mxl-st2110-gateway 1.0.14. Channels that read another function's domain already had an MXL instance of their own and were not affected. Each output channel's sync group now lives on its own MXL instance of the readers' domain, which holds no readers. This costs one more MXL instance per running output channel (an idle inotify watcher thread and a few file descriptors). New unit test: sync groups are created and released while another thread releases readers of the same domain. It crashed in 50 of 50 runs with the group on the shared instance and passed 50 of 50 with the fix. Lab (DeckLink IP 100G, three 1080p50 inputs, two outputs fed from this function's own input flows, 40 IS-05 re-routes of the outputs): no crash, 0 dropped frames.
- An output channel's teardown (preroll reset, failed bring-up, restart, stop) now stops scheduled playback first, waits until no DeckLink completion or audio callback is running, and only then frees the sync group, the playback session and the MXL readers. Before, it freed the sync group before stopping playback, so a completion callback still in `scheduleGrain` could wait on a freed group; the audio callback could read readers being cleared. The DeckLink SDK does not promise that `StopScheduledPlayback` waits for a running callback, so each callback now holds a per-channel lock for its whole run and the teardown takes that lock after the stop.
- Built on MXL `release/v1.1` at `218ddaa` (was tag `v1.1.0`, `8f3f1096`), the revision mxl-st2110-gateway 1.0.15 and the other media functions use. It adds the writer fix `b8ec4bb6` (a reused ring slot no longer shows the previous grain's `validSlices`) and the reader check `d66dcf3c` (`Instance::getFlowReader` checks the flow id). The shared-memory format is unchanged. The image label `io.dmf.mxl.revision` is `218ddaa0a08c12ffe75fc475ae65aa3d9eef16d7`; the Docker build and CI clone the branch and check out that commit. No settings change.

## 1.0.2

- MXL receivers accept domains created after the start. The IS-05 constraints listed `mxl_domain_id` as an `enum` of the domains found at startup (nmos-cpp writes a one-element `enum` when there was exactly one), so a PATCH naming a later domain, such as the output domain of a production deployed afterwards, failed with 400 until the pod restarted. The constraint no longer lists domains; an activation (or an `auto` resolution) that names an unknown domain or flow scans `MXL_DOMAIN_SCAN_PATH` again and is refused only if the domain is still not there. No settings change.

## 1.0.1

- Hardware-timestamp recalibration steps to a delta above the 1 ms gate when the next recalibration is again above the gate in the same direction. Before, every such delta was rejected and the timestamps of a card without PTP lock drifted without bound (5 ms per minute on the lab DeckLink IP 100G; 315 s after six weeks). A single jump is still rejected. New log event `hw_clock_recalibration_stepped`.

- SPECIFICATION §3.10 and the Running section of the README now describe a completed SIGTERM as exit 143, matching the process. `v1.0.0` already behaved that way.

## 1.0.0

Stable platform contract for settings, HTTP APIs, and shutdown. A later breaking change needs 2.0.0.

- Output domain: `MXL_OUTPUT_DOMAIN_DIR` (default `/Volumes/mxl/mxl-decklink`) and `MXL_OUTPUT_DOMAIN_ID`. `MXL_DOMAIN_PATH` remains an alias of the directory. An existing `domain_def.json` with a different id is not overwritten (exit 78).
- `MXL_DOMAIN_SCAN_PATH` defaults to `/Volumes/mxl`.
- `MXL_HISTORY_DURATION_MS` is written to `options.json` only when that file does not already exist.
- `NMOS_SEED` derives node, device, source, sender, receiver, and default domain ids with UUIDv5 (`mxl-decklink/<seed>/<role>` under the DNS namespace). Without a seed, ids stay derived from the DeckLink persistent id.
- `NMOS_TAGS` (JSON object) is added to the node and device. Group hints are unchanged.
- `NMOS_DNS_SD` defaults to false: no DNS-SD browse and no mDNS advertisement (`pri` and `highest_pri` at `INT_MAX`). `NMOS_QUERY_ADDRESS` defaults to the registry address; `NMOS_QUERY_PORT` defaults to the registration port + 1.
- `NMOS_HOST_ADDRESS` is the IP literal announced in IS-04 and IS-05. Default is the first non-loopback IPv4. Hostnames, `0.0.0.0`, and loopback are rejected.
- `/readyz` stays 503 until the node is registered when a registry is configured or DNS-SD is on.
- SIGTERM exits **143** after stopping media, asking nmos-cpp to DELETE registry resources, and, when `MXL_CLEANUP_ON_EXIT=true`, removing only this function's output domain directory.
- State file defaults to `$CONFIG_DIR/mxl-decklink.json` (`CONFIG_DIR` default `/config`). `GET /api/v1/config/export` and `POST /api/v1/config/import` read and replace that file. There are no secrets in the document. IS-05 `master_enable` and flow ids are written there when the environment does not already pin those keys (`CHx_MXL_ACTIVE`, `CHx_AFn_MXL_ACTIVE`).
- Prometheus series use the `mxl_decklink_` prefix.
- The container image runs as uid/gid 1000. The `video` group is added only to open `/dev/blackmagic`.
